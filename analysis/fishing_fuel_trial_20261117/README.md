# Paired fishing-vessel fuel sensitivity, 2023

`run_fuel_trial.py` replays the historical vessel model and evaluates five arms on each of 500 paired draws. Run from the repository root with `C:/CONDA/python -B analysis/fishing_fuel_trial_20261117/run_fuel_trial.py`. Production inputs are read from the historical inventory. Outputs are written here.

- `Operating_Priors.csv` lists the triangular bounds, modes and basis for each parameter. `Operating_Draws.csv` records the accepted operating draws. The global pooled priors describe exploratory effective annual use per eligible inventory vessel. The underlying registry, fleet reconstruction and vessel age eligibility are carried over from the historical model.
- `Original` replays the historical model. `Days_only` replaces annual days. `Hours_only` replaces main and auxiliary engine runtime and redistributes main-engine hours across operating states while retaining legacy load factors. `Load_only` replaces the load factors, recalculates diesel specific fuel consumption at those loads and retains legacy total and fishing main-engine hours. Existing fishing hours are split between setting and hauling using the sampled proportions. `All` applies all three replacements. Installed power, fleet composition, catch, fuel density and allocation weights are held fixed within pairs.
- In the hours arm, engine-on waiting time carries the original nonfishing load until the load block is switched on. The arms are contrasts against the old model. Their percentage changes do not sum to the joint change. The output percentiles describe the specified priors.
- The `Global` rows report fuel attributed to the five market tuna species (million litres) by fleet class. The benchmark rows use the existing SI match for Pacific and Indian Ocean purse seine (`PS23`), Indian Ocean purse seine (`PS23I`) and Pacific longline (`LL23`). The fleet volume to catch ratio uses the same modeled catch denominator in every arm. Group-specific catches for diesel and gasoline longline are unavailable because grid catches lack engine type. Use the combined longline rows for intensity comparisons and the separate rows to compare class fuel volumes.
- The replay begins in 2023, so its original arm preserves the model equations but uses a different random-number sequence than the archived 1950–2025 inventory. `Archived_model_FUI_L_t` records the published-analysis input separately. The ISSF survey is an external check by gear and broad scope. It was not used to tune the priors or supply fuel volumes.

## Parameter sources

- Purse-seine activity, shaft power and separate main and auxiliary consumption are from Basurko et al. (2022), Tables 1 and 3. The monitored vessel gives a mean main-engine shaft power of 2,423.3 kW while cruising and 293.8 kW while fishing, relative to 4,564 kW installed. This is a single monitored vessel. Port time is excluded when deriving the at-sea hours. [Full text](https://www.bmis-bycatch.org/system/files/zotero_attachments/library_1/652FZ6N3%20-%20Basurko%20et%20al.%20-%202022%20-%20Fuel%20consumption%20of%20free-swimming%20school%20versus%20FA.pdf)
- Purse-seine annual sea days draw on the Parker basin means retained in the project parameter matrix and the FAO Spain chapter, Table 6 (320 mean annual sea days for the Spanish industrial fleet in 2016). Those case values anchor the scenario and do not measure effective days per vessel across the global registry. [Parker et al.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959652614004776) · [FAO report text posted by a co-author](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/394192303_Techno-economic_performance_review_of_selected_fishing_fleets_in_Europe)
- Diesel longline time allocations draw on Lu et al. (2024), section 2.3.3, and the Australian fresh-fish technical case, section A1.3.12. The previous trial supplies the global annual-day and transit-only priors. Load for longline setting, hauling and drift is an engineering range. [AIS field analysis](https://www.mdpi.com/2410-3888/9/2/66) · [Australian technical case](https://wedocs.unep.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/d636f5e3-928d-47f1-badd-f4ba3967eb4a/content) · [EEA Navigation Chapter, section 3.4.2](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/analysis/publications/emep-eea-guidebook-2023/part-b-sectoral-guidance-chapters/1-energy/1-a-combustion/1-a-3-d-navigation/@@download/file)
- Outboard longline bounds are exploratory. EPA's outboard test cycle is general technical context. No corresponding annual activity or fishing-stage load survey of small gasoline tuna longliners was verified, so these cases are sensitivity bounds and cannot establish a revised best estimate for the small-boat fleet. [EPA NR-005d](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P10081RV.TXT)
- The 2023 ISSF gear averages are 496.3 L/t for purse seine and 1,523.3 L/t for longline. The ISSF study does not give a benchmark for the model's gasoline outboard subset. [ISSF 2025-03, Table 10](https://www.iss-foundation.org/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/2025/03/ISSF-2025-03-Fuel-Consumption-and-Greenhouse-Gas-Emissions-from-Global-Tuna-Fisheries-2024-Updated-Report.pdf)

Fig. 2 | Spatial patterns and historical trends reveal contrasting emission intensities. a, Allocated GHG divided by catch across 1970–1990, 1991–2005 and 2006–2024. Colors share a logarithmic CEI scale; endpoint colors denote values outside its limits. Species symbols identify at least 50% of cell catch, and boundary colors identify RFMOs. b, Trends in CPUE, CEI and GHG by gear, requiring at least ten observed years after 1970. Percentage trends are annual slopes divided by temporal means. CPUE uses assumed annual technology corrections of 1.86% for LL and 4.26% for PS. Blue and red denote declining and increasing trends. Hooks and sets are separate effort units. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; CEI, catch-based emission intensity; CPUE, catch per unit effort; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; SKJ, skipjack; YFT, yellowfin; BET, bigeye; ALB, albacore; SBT, southern bluefin tuna; RFMOs, regional fisheries management organizations.

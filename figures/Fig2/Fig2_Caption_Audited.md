Fig. 2 | Spatial differences and historical trends in catch-based emission intensity. a, Grid CEI across three eras, calculated as allocated GHG divided by catch. Colours share a logarithmic scale; out-of-range values use endpoint colours. Species symbols denote at least 50% of cell catch. Boundary colours identify RFMOs. b, CPUE, CEI and GHG trends by gear, requiring at least ten observed years from 1970. Percentage trends are linear slopes divided by temporal means. CPUE is adjusted for assumed technological change at 1.86% yr⁻¹ for LL and 4.26% yr⁻¹ for PS. Blue and red indicate declining and increasing trends. Spatial records end in 2024; hooks and sets remain separate effort units.

Supplementary Fig. 18 | External fuel benchmarks distinguish magnitude and accounting scope. a,b, Model and literature fuel intensities for PS and LL, matched by activity year and broad region; labels give central values. c, Descriptive relative gaps for the same six comparisons. Hollow symbols identify the calibration-linked 2009 PS survey; filled symbols identify external fuel outcomes. d, Model combustion, upstream fuel supply and other LCA components in the 2023 matched scopes, with survey combustion shown separately as orange diamonds. LL and PS facets have separate x scales; the survey points do not represent full-LCA totals. e, Spanish PS full-LCA context in 2003; the delivery boundary and shared sources prevent an independent matched-system validation. Lines connect comparisons, not uncertainty intervals. Fleet samples and catch coverage remain imperfectly matched. Sources and scope decisions are given in S6.4 and Supplementary Table 10. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; LCA, life-cycle assessment; ALB, albacore.

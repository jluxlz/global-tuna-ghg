Supplementary Fig. 23 | Regional input diagnostics and archived scenario checks. a, Indian Ocean BET (1975–2024) and historical YFT (1950–2020) total-biomass fits with posterior 95% ranges. b, Maximum rank Rhat and minimum bulk/tail ESS across monitored states and parameters. c, Archived paired GHG comparisons for 100% and 80% FMSY, fuels and combined measures in conditional regional fleet components. These archived contrasts use their original fixed-2024-catch target, whereas the current C/F/T experiments in Extended Data Fig. 9 use an MSY-based C rule. BET uses a 2024 reference state; YFT uses its 2020 historical state. d, Advancing historical YFT with subsequent reported catches yields negative biomass when 2024 removals are applied. Negative values diagnose model incompatibility and are never used to drive simulations. Passing MCMC convergence criteria does not establish current stock validity. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; BET, bigeye; YFT, yellowfin; MSY, maximum sustainable yield; FMSY, fishing mortality at MSY; ESS, effective sample size; MCMC, Markov chain Monte Carlo.

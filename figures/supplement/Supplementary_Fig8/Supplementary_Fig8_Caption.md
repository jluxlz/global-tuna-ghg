Supplementary Fig. 8 | Production JABBA chain trajectories. Retained draws of r, K and initial depletion ψ for each species and each of four chains. K uses the scaled internal JAGS units and should not be read directly as Mt. Retained draws per chain are SKJ 10,000, YFT 8,000, BET 10,000, ALB 12,500 and SBT 10,000. Actual chain identities are retained from the JAGS arrays. Trace overlap is interpreted alongside the rank Rhat, bulk/tail ESS48 and independent-fit checks. r denotes intrinsic annual growth, K carrying capacity and ψ initial depletion. Abbreviations: JABBA, Just Another Bayesian Biomass Assessment; JAGS, Just Another Gibbs Sampler; MCMC, Markov chain Monte Carlo; ESS, effective sample size; Mt, million metric tons; SKJ, skipjack; YFT, yellowfin; BET, bigeye; ALB, albacore; SBT, southern bluefin tuna.

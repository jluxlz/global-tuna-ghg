Supplementary Fig. 11 | Consolidation schedule and equal-power accounting. a, Implemented and remaining shares of the prescribed 2025–2050 transition; marked years are schedule milestones. b, The corresponding operating-time factor, with shading indicating its prescribed reduction from one. The factor reaches 0.757 in 2050; this is an assumption, not an observed efficiency gain. c, Equal-width bands show a 1,000 kW accounting example with identical retired and replacement power. Bands do not encode vessel counts. Larger templates remain within organization–gear groups; forced retirement has a separate effort-conservation rule. The operating-time factor is applied once and does not prescribe an equal reduction in whole-fleet GHG. Abbreviation: GHG, greenhouse gas.

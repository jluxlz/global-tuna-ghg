Supplementary Fig. 14 | External benchmarking of fuel use and GHG emission estimates. a,b, Fishing-vessel fuel intensity for PS and LL, matched by activity year and broad geographic domain. Filled symbols show model central estimates; hollow symbols literature values. Lines connect descriptive comparisons, not uncertainty intervals. c, Model combustion-only GHG versus the ISSF 2023 diesel case. Upstream fuel supply is excluded from both plotted estimates. d, Spanish PS full-LCA context in 2003; the literature includes delivery to Galicia and shares background sources, so the systems are not matched. The 2009 PS survey family informs model operating-day assumptions; the 2023 survey supplies independent fuel outcomes. Catch species, fleet samples and attribution remain imperfectly matched.

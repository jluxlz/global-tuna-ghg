Extended Data Fig. 6 | Mechanisms underlying GHG differences at comparable cumulative catch. a, Exact accounting contributions for F/C, T/C and T/F across nine matched futures, as percentages of reference whole-fleet cumulative GHG. Negative contributions lower emissions; comparisons have separate x scales. All eight terms close to each whole-fleet difference. b, Paired ratios of mean annual species biomass. c, Paired ratios of period physical effort per metric ton, using summed effort divided by summed catch within each species. Dashed references mark unity; b,c use logarithmic axes. Points show nine futures, boxes medians and interquartile ranges, and whiskers 1.5 interquartile ranges. Fractions can differ between matched designs; these are model and accounting contrasts, not isolated threshold effects. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; C, constant catch; F, constant harvest rate; T, biomass-threshold harvest rate; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; SKJ, skipjack; YFT, yellowfin; BET, bigeye; ALB, albacore; SBT, southern bluefin tuna.

Extended Data Fig. 1 | System boundary of tuna-fishery GHG accounting. The boundary includes upstream inputs and fishing activities in coastal and distant-water fisheries. Inputs cover energy, materials, infrastructure, equipment, shipbuilding, fishing-gear manufacture and LL bait production. Activities include transit, rigging, fish finding, fishing, onboard processing and freezing. Distant-water operations also include at-sea transhipment and transport to port.

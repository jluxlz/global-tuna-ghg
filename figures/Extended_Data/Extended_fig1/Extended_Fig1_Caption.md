Extended Data Fig. 1 | System boundary of tuna-fishery GHG accounting. The schematic locates upstream materials and equipment, vessel construction and maintenance, fishing operations, onboard handling and freezing, bait, and transport to port. The quantified inventory includes the fuel, gear, vessel-material, bait and refrigerant processes defined in M2. No separate vessel- or gear-end-of-life process is calculated. Post-landing processing, distribution, retail and consumption are outside the calculation. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; TP, transshipment carrier; SA, support or auxiliary vessel.

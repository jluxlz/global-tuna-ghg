Extended Data Fig. 2 | Historical inventory and future stock–fleet comparisons. Registry and capacity records describe the fleet; catch and technology-adjusted CPUE inform exploratory stock models. Historical CEI analysis separates effort per catch from GHG per effort. The fixed-catch target simulation combines quota-related  and predicted fuel-cost  effort factors, using prescribed biomass trajectories with a lower limit. The feasible-catch simulation converts stock-dependent targets to effort demand, limits activity by demand, capacity and costs, and updates biomass using realised removals. Catch shares connect inventory landings with stock removals. Actual operating days enter GHG calculations, including the LN₂ demand proxy. Each simulation compares measures with its own BAU across 81 configurations.

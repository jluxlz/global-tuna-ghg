Extended Data Fig. 2 | Historical inventory and unified HCR–fleet comparisons. Registry and capacity data inform the vessel inventory; catch and technology-adjusted CPUE inform exploratory stock models. C-, F- and T-type harvest control rules generate targets from beginning-of-year biomass. All rules share stock production, finite harvest pulses, demand, capacity and fuel-cost constraints, realized-catch feedback and vessel GHG accounting. Each of 81 exogenous futures is crossed with three HCRs and eight technical subsets. Technical reductions use each rule’s own no-transition reference; matched-catch comparisons adjust HCR fractions using catch alone. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; CEI, catch-based emission intensity; CPUE, catch per unit effort; HCR, harvest control rule; RFMO, regional fisheries management organization; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; WEO, World Energy Outlook.

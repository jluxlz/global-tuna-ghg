Extended Data Fig. 4 | Catch and intensity contributions to spatial emission changes. a, Mean annual catch and GHG by era, with counts of distinct 5° grid–gear cells harmonized as in Fig. 2. b, Contributions from grids observed in both periods, newly recorded grids and grids absent later. c, Additive LMDI catch and CEI contributions within common grids, on an annual basis for full and sufficient-data samples. Contributions sum without a residual interaction term. d, Catch-weighted CEI for LL and PS cells combined (Full grid), LL cells (LL grid) and PS cells (PS grid). All three series use positive-catch, positive-GHG records and the same ratio-of-totals definition. The pooled series varies with gear composition and is distinct from the within-cell intensity contribution in c. The final era includes 19 observed years through 2024. Newly recorded grids can reflect changes in activity or reporting coverage. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; CEI, catch-based emission intensity; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; LMDI, logarithmic-mean Divisia index.

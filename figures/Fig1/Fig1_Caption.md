Fig. 1 | Historical tuna-fishery GHG emissions differ across fleets and regions. a, Annual emissions and process shares, with the central 90% selected-parameter Monte Carlo range from 500 draws. Fuel includes combustion and upstream supply. b, Annual gear emissions and matched-catch CEI; gray early segments indicate limited coverage. Carriers and support vessels have no catch denominator. c, Species catch, allocated GHG and CEI on positive-catch, positive-emission records. Bubble area represents CEI. d, Emissions assigned by fishing location; bars sum fleet-year medians and whiskers show paired-draw 5th–95th percentiles. Spatial records span 1951–2024 within the 1950–2025 inventory. Vessel-affiliation allocation is in Extended Data Fig. 3a. Annual-median sums and paired cumulative medians are distinct summaries. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; CEI, catch-based emission intensity; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; TP, transshipment carrier; SA, support or auxiliary vessel; SKJ, skipjack; YFT, yellowfin; BET, bigeye; ALB, albacore; SBT, southern bluefin tuna; WCPFC, Western and Central Pacific Fisheries Commission; IATTC, Inter-American Tropical Tuna Commission; ICCAT, International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas; IOTC, Indian Ocean Tuna Commission; CCSBT, Commission for the Conservation of Southern Bluefin Tuna.

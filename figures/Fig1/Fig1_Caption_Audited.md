Fig. 1 | Historical tuna-fishery GHG emissions and differences among fleets. a, Annual fleet GHG and six lifecycle process shares; fuel includes combustion and upstream supply. Shading shows the central 90% selected-parameter Monte Carlo range. b, Annual emissions by gear, with quantiles calculated after summing paired draws. Dotted CEI lines use matched catch and allocated GHG; grey early segments mark limited coverage. Carriers and support vessels have no catch denominator. c, Species catch and catch-allocated GHG on positive-catch, positive-emission records. Bubble area indicates CEI; the dashed line is pooled matched-sample CEI. d, Emissions assigned by fishing location to RFMOs, including zero-target-catch effort records. Bars sum allocated fleet-year medians; whiskers are cumulative paired-draw 5th–95th percentiles. Spatial records cover 1951–2024, within the 1950–2025 fleet inventory. Allocation by vessel affiliation, including carriers and support vessels, is shown in Extended Data Fig. 3a. Annual median sums and the median of paired cumulative draws are distinct summaries.

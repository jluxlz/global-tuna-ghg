Fig. 4 | Quota rules and fuel pathways affect stocks, effort and GHG. a, Beginning-of-year biomass relative to BMSY under constant-catch (C), constant-harvest-rate (F) and biomass-threshold (T) rules without technical transition. Lines and ribbons show medians and 5th–95th percentiles over 81 exogenous futures per rule. Horizontal references mark B/BMSY = 1 and T-type trigger/closure proxies of 0.8/0.2. b, Raw fuel-cost responses relative to 2023 by pathway and region. c, Realized physical effort and effort per catch for LL and PS; logarithmic y axes, with the same summaries as a. d, Raw cost responses for eight fuels relative to same-year EU MGO. e, Lifecycle GHG per engine-kWh multiplied by the raw cost response; this analytical intensity does not include fleet-capacity constraints. f, First year when liquid-nitrogen refrigeration has lower GHG than R22; crosses above 2050 mean no crossover. HVO/FAME price paths use the prescribed positive inverse carbon-price relationship; fixed-price reruns appear in Supplementary Fig. 21. B is biomass; BMSY is the Schaefer biomass reference K/2, with K carrying capacity. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; HCR, harvest control rule; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; MSY, maximum sustainable yield; WEO, World Energy Outlook; STEPS, Stated Policies Scenario; APS, Announced Pledges Scenario; NZE, Net Zero Emissions by 2050 Scenario; EU, European Union; MGO, marine gas oil; HFO, heavy fuel oil; LNG, liquefied natural gas; HVO, hydrotreated vegetable oil; FAME, fatty acid methyl esters; H2, hydrogen; H2G, green hydrogen; NH3, ammonia; NH3G, green ammonia; LN2, liquid nitrogen; SKJ, skipjack; YFT, yellowfin; BET, bigeye; ALB, albacore; SBT, southern bluefin tuna.

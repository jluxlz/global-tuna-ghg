Fig. 5 | Quota design shapes production and technical mitigation. a, Annual no-transition GHG medians and 5th–95th scenario percentiles. b, Cumulative catch, GHG, period CEI and lowest beginning-of-2050 stock B/BMSY. c, Cumulative fuel abatement. d, Single and combined technical measures; c,d use each rule’s no-transition reference. e, Nine C/F/T triples matched within 1% cumulative catch: linked gray/blue/green points show C/F/T GHG; adjacent facets show paired reductions on separate x scales. Rows identify WEO and rounded annual technology creep. Fractions are selected using catch alone; matched F/T fractions can differ. Cumulative quantities cover 2025–2050; a–d summarize 81 futures per rule. Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers extend 1.5 interquartile ranges. Ranges are not confidence intervals. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; CEI, catch-based emission intensity; C, constant catch; F, constant harvest rate; T, biomass-threshold harvest rate; BMSY, biomass at maximum sustainable yield; WEO, World Energy Outlook; STEPS, Stated Policies Scenario; APS, Announced Pledges Scenario; NZE, Net Zero Emissions by 2050 Scenario.

Fig. 5 | Achievable catch changes mitigation rankings and quota-related intensity benefits. a, Annual BAU medians and 5th–95th scenario percentiles for fixed-catch targets and feasible catch. b, Paired 2050 reductions relative to each configuration's own BAU. c, Quota versus fuel reductions; the diagonal marks equality. d, Changes in cumulative catch, cumulative GHG and period CEI over 2025–2050 under feasible-catch quota-only runs. e, Nine additional comparisons with cumulative catch matched within 1%; median F-type cumulative GHG is 19.4% lower. C-type uses 65% MSY; F-type fractions are adjusted. Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges, whiskers extend to 1.5 interquartile ranges and points show all configurations. Feasible-catch simulations update biomass using realised catch and apply a 365-day bound. Scenario ranges describe variation across assumptions, not confidence intervals. Comparable-catch results apply to the adjusted rules. Fuel and quota comparisons and regional technology effects are in Supplementary Figs. 9, 11 and 16.

Fig. 3 | Effort requirements and emissions per effort jointly shape CEI. a, Log trends in standardized effort per metric ton and GHG per standardized effort for 2,715 grid–gear–era fits. Bubble area represents GHG; arrows join emission-weighted species-group averages across 1970–1990, 1991–2005 and 2006–2024. The diagonal marks zero CEI trend. b,c, LL and PS factors by RFMO: gray bubbles represent grid–era records; fixed-size colored symbols identify regional ratios of totals and successive eras. LL units are hooks per 100 t catch and t CO₂e per 100 standardized hooks; PS units are sets per metric ton and t CO₂e per standardized set. Panel b uses a shared zoomed y range; all grid records remain in regional calculations. CCSBT PS has separate scales. Effort includes assumed technology correction; observations end in 2024. Abbreviations: GHG, greenhouse gas; CEI, catch-based emission intensity; LL, longline; PS, purse seine; RFMO, regional fisheries management organization; SKJ, skipjack; YFT, yellowfin; BET, bigeye; ALB, albacore; SBT, southern bluefin tuna; WCPFC, Western and Central Pacific Fisheries Commission; IATTC, Inter-American Tropical Tuna Commission; ICCAT, International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas; IOTC, Indian Ocean Tuna Commission; CCSBT, Commission for the Conservation of Southern Bluefin Tuna; CO₂e, carbon dioxide equivalent.

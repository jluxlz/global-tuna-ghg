Fig. 3 | Effort requirements and emissions per effort jointly shape CEI. a, Grid–gear log trends in standardised effort per tonne (x) and GHG per standardised effort (y). Bubble area represents GHG; arrows connect emission-weighted average positions of dominant-species grid groups across three eras. Zero axes separate factor signs; x + y = 0 separates increasing and decreasing CEI. b,c, LL and PS trajectories by RFMO. Grey bubbles are grid–era ratios; coloured symbols are regional ratios of totals, joined in era order. LL units are hooks per 100 t catch and t CO₂e per 100 standardised hooks; PS units are sets per tonne and t CO₂e per 100 standardised sets. Regional symbol area indicates era GHG. CCSBT PS uses separate scales. Effort includes the assumed technology correction; observations end in 2024.
